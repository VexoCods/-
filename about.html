At Horizon Properties, we connect people with extraordinary homes and smart investments. Integrity, transparency, and client satisfaction are at the heart of everything we do.

Since 2004

A specialist practice, deliberately small

Horizon Properties was founded on a simple observation: the most interesting houses are rarely served well by a generalist agency. We take on a limited number of instructions at any one time, so each one is handled by an advisor who knows the street, the architect and the last three comparable sales.

That approach has taken us from a single office in Austin to mandates across 38 cities, covering waterfront houses, hillside retreats, penthouses and land. We advise private clients, family offices and developers on value, timing and structure — and we are equally happy to tell you when the right answer is to wait.

Every campaign is art-directed as a piece of editorial: commissioned photography, written copy and a considered placement schedule rather than an automatic listing.
