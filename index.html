Our Story

More than coffee.

Caffeine Cove began with a single espresso machine and a stubborn belief that a neighbourhood deserves better than a rushed cup. Ten years on, we still roast in small batches, bake before sunrise and greet most of our guests by name.

It's a place for slow mornings, creative conversations and unhurried afternoons — where the food is made properly and nobody minds if you stay a while.
